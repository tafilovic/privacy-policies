Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Tabak Development ("We", "Us" or "Our") collects, uses and shares information when You use the School timetable application for Android and iOS (the "Application"), and which rights You have.

In short: the Application works without an account and then keeps all data on Your device only. If You sign in, Your timetables are stored on Our server so they can be synced between Your devices and shared with the members of Your Plus Family. Plus is an optional paid upgrade bought through Google Play. We do not show ads, do not sell data and do not use analytics or tracking.

Who is responsible

Tabak Development (Šemsudin Tafilović) is the controller of the personal data processed through the Application. Contact: tabakdevelopment@gmail.com.

A parent who creates timetables for a child is responsible for the information they enter about the child; We process it on their behalf to provide the Application.

A teacher who keeps a class book in the Application (students and their grades) is responsible for that data, together with their school where the school's rules apply. For this data We act as a processor on the teacher's behalf: We store and sync it only so the teacher can use it, and nobody else sees it.

Data We process

Without an account

Everything You enter – profiles, subjects, lessons, bell times, tests, homework and other events, grades and the class book – is stored only in the Application's database on Your device. We do not receive it. Reminders are planned and shown by Your device.

With an account (Google or email sign-in)

  • Account data: Your email address, an internal user ID and, with Google sign-in, Your Google account ID and the name of Your Google profile. With email sign-in We send a one-time code to Your email address.

  • Timetable content: profile names (for example a child's first name or nickname), class and school name, avatar emoji and colour, subjects, teacher names, rooms, lessons, bell times, tests, homework and other events with their titles, dates, notes and reminder settings.

  • Grades: grades You enter for a subject, with their weight, type, date and an optional note.

  • Class book (teacher timetables): names of the teacher's classes or groups, names, roll numbers and optional notes of their students, and the grades the teacher gives them. The class book is visible only to the teacher who owns the timetable – not to people the timetable is shared with.

  • Purchases: when You buy Plus, Google Play handles the payment. We receive from Google Play the product, the purchase token, the order number, the purchase state and the start and end dates, so We can confirm the purchase, unlock Plus on Your devices and react to renewals, cancellations and refunds. We never see Your payment details. With Plus Family, the people who join the buyer's Plus Family get Plus too, and the Application shows them the buyer's name.

  • Plus Family and sharing data: who is in Your Plus Family, the one-time invite codes used to join it (valid for 7 days), and which family members can see or edit which of Your timetables. People in the same Plus Family see each other's name or email address, so a timetable owner can choose who gets access.

  • Device data: an installation ID, a push token (Firebase Cloud Messaging) so that changes from family members reach Your device quickly, the platform, the app version and when the account and the device were last active.

  • Technical logs: Our server keeps short-lived request logs (for example IP address, time and error codes) to run the service securely and fix errors.

Permissions on Your device

  • Notifications – to show reminders for tests, homework and other events.
  • Camera – only when You scan the QR code of a Plus Family invite. Images are processed on Your device and not stored or sent.
  • Internet – to sync when You are signed in.
  • Files – only a timetable file You choose to import or open with the Application. No access to Your other files.

Timetable files: when You share a timetable "for classmates", the Application creates a file on Your device with the bell times, subjects and lessons (no grades, class book, names of family members or account data) and hands it to the app You choose, for example a chat app. The file does not pass through Our server.

When You update from the previous version of the Application, it can read that version's timetable database on Your device to import Your timetables. The old data is not changed or sent anywhere unless You sign in.

Why We use the data

  • To provide the Application: sign-in, storing and syncing Your timetables, sharing them with the members of Your Plus Family, and sending sync signals to Your devices (performance of the contract, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • To sell and provide Plus: checking purchases with Google Play, unlocking Plus for You and Your Plus Family, renewals, cancellations and refunds (performance of the contract, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR), and keeping purchase records as the law requires (legal obligation, Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR).
  • To keep the service secure and working, including seeing which app versions are still in use (legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

We do not use the data for advertising, profiling or automated decisions.

Service providers

We use these providers to run the Application. They process data only on Our behalf:

  • netcup GmbH – hosting of Our server (database, sign-in and server functions) in Germany, European Union.
  • Brevo (Sendinblue SAS) – sending the one-time sign-in codes by email, European Union.
  • Google – Google sign-in (if You choose it), Google Play for purchases of Plus, Firebase Cloud Messaging for sync signals, and storage of Our encrypted backups in Google Drive (Google cannot read their content). Google may process data outside the European Union under appropriate safeguards (EU Standard Contractual Clauses).
  • Supabase – Our previous host in the European Union; a copy of the data stays there until 10 November 2026 and is then deleted.

We do not sell, rent or share personal data with anyone else, except when required by law.

Retention and deletion

  • Data on Your device stays until You delete it in the Application (Settings → Delete all data) or uninstall it.
  • Your account data is kept as long as Your account exists.
  • When someone leaves a Plus Family or is removed from it, they lose access to the timetables shared with them through it.
  • Purchase records are kept while needed to provide Plus and handle refunds, and as long as tax and accounting laws require, even after the account is deleted. A subscription is managed in Google Play: deleting the account does not cancel it – cancel it in Google Play.
  • Deleting Your account permanently deletes Your account and every timetable You created, including their lessons, events, grades and class book, also for the people You shared them with. You lose access to timetables others shared with You; theirs stay with them. You can delete the account in the Application (account → Delete account) or request it on the account deletion page. Deletion is done within 30 days of a request by email; in the Application it is immediate.
  • Signing out removes the account's data from the device; it stays in Your account.
  • Technical logs are deleted automatically after a short period.

Your rights

Under the GDPR and similar laws You have the right to access, correct, delete and export Your data, to restrict or object to processing, and to complain to a data protection authority. You can correct and delete data directly in the Application. For other requests write to tabakdevelopment@gmail.com.

Children

The Application helps students, parents and teachers organise school. It contains no ads and no chat. Plus is an optional purchase through Google Play; on a child's Google account, Google asks the parent to approve it (Family Link). We collect only the data needed for the timetable – no birth dates, photos or location. A child's timetable is usually created and shared by a parent. If You are a parent and believe Your child gave Us personal data without Your consent, contact Us and We will delete it.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Access on the server is limited to the members of each timetable, and the class book to the timetable's owner. Sign-in tokens are kept in the secure storage of Your device. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, but We use commonly accepted measures to protect Your data.
