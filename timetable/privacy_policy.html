Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Tabak Development ("We", "Us" or "Our") collects, uses and shares information when You use the School timetable application for Android and iOS (the "Application"), and which rights You have.

In short: the Application works without an account and then keeps all data on Your device only. If You sign in, Your timetables are stored on Our server so they can be synced between Your devices and shared with people You invite. We do not show ads, do not sell data and do not use analytics or tracking.

Who is responsible

Tabak Development (Šemsudin Tafilović) is the controller of the personal data processed through the Application. Contact: tabakdevelopment@gmail.com.

A parent who creates timetables for a child is responsible for the information they enter about the child; We process it on their behalf to provide the Application.

Data We process

Without an account

Everything You enter – profiles, subjects, lessons, bell times, tests, homework and other events – is stored only in the Application's database on Your device. We do not receive it. Reminders are planned and shown by Your device.

With an account (Google or email sign-in)

  • Account data: Your email address, an internal user ID and, with Google sign-in, Your Google account ID and the name of Your Google profile. With email sign-in We send a one-time code to Your email address.

  • Timetable content: profile names (for example a child's first name or nickname), class and school name, avatar emoji and colour, subjects, teacher names, rooms, lessons, bell times, tests, homework and other events with their titles, dates, notes and reminder settings.

  • Sharing data: who has access to which timetable and with which role, and invite codes. Members of a shared timetable see each other's name or email address.

  • Device data: an installation ID, a push token (Firebase Cloud Messaging) so that changes from family members reach Your device quickly, the platform and the app version.

  • Technical logs: Our server keeps short-lived request logs (for example IP address, time and error codes) to run the service securely and fix errors.

Permissions on Your device

  • Notifications – to show reminders for tests, homework and other events.
  • Camera – only when You scan the QR code of an invite. Images are processed on Your device and not stored or sent.
  • Internet – to sync when You are signed in.

When You update from the previous version of the Application, it can read that version's timetable database on Your device to import Your timetables. The old data is not changed or sent anywhere unless You sign in.

Why We use the data

  • To provide the Application: sign-in, storing and syncing Your timetables, sharing them with people You invite, and sending sync signals to Your devices (performance of the contract, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • To keep the service secure and working (legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

We do not use the data for advertising, profiling or automated decisions.

Service providers

We use these providers to run the Application. They process data only on Our behalf:

  • Supabase – database, sign-in and server functions, hosted in the European Union.
  • Google – Google sign-in (if You choose it) and Firebase Cloud Messaging for sync signals. Google may process data outside the European Union under appropriate safeguards (EU Standard Contractual Clauses).

We do not sell, rent or share personal data with anyone else, except when required by law.

Retention and deletion

  • Data on Your device stays until You delete it in the Application (Settings → Delete all data) or uninstall it.
  • Your account data is kept as long as Your account exists.
  • Deleting Your account permanently deletes Your account and every timetable You created, including their lessons and events, also for the people You shared them with. You lose access to timetables others shared with You; theirs stay with them. You can delete the account in the Application (account → Delete account) or request it on the account deletion page. Deletion is done within 30 days of a request by email; in the Application it is immediate.
  • Signing out removes the account's data from the device; it stays in Your account.
  • Technical logs are deleted automatically after a short period.

Your rights

Under the GDPR and similar laws You have the right to access, correct, delete and export Your data, to restrict or object to processing, and to complain to a data protection authority. You can correct and delete data directly in the Application. For other requests write to tabakdevelopment@gmail.com.

Children

The Application helps students, parents and teachers organise school. It contains no ads, no in-app purchases and no chat. We collect only the data needed for the timetable – no birth dates, photos or location. A child's timetable is usually created and shared by a parent. If You are a parent and believe Your child gave Us personal data without Your consent, contact Us and We will delete it.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Access on the server is limited to the members of each timetable. Sign-in tokens are kept in the secure storage of Your device. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, but We use commonly accepted measures to protect Your data.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. The current version is always on this page with its "Last updated" date.
