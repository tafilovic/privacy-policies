Delete your account and data

Deleting your School timetable account permanently removes:

  • your account (email address, user ID, Google account link),
  • every timetable you created, with its subjects, lessons, bell times, tests, homework and other events – also for the people you shared it with,
  • your access to timetables others shared with you (their timetables stay with them),
  • the push tokens of your devices.

This cannot be undone. Short-lived technical logs are deleted automatically after a short period. Data stored only on your device without an account is not on our servers; delete it in the app (Settings → Delete all data) or by uninstalling the app.

In the app (immediate)

  1. Open School timetable and sign in.
  2. Tap your avatar in the top bar to open the account sheet.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Without the app

Send an email from the address of your account to tabakdevelopment@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my School timetable account". We delete the account and its data within 30 days and confirm by email.
